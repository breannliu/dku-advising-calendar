# Subscribe to DKU Academic Support Calendars

You may subscribe to the DKU Academic Support calendars in your own calendar app. These calendars include academic dates, ASP workshops, and CRLA training sessions. Please subscribe only to the calendars that are relevant to you.

## How to subscribe

### Mac Calendar

1. Open **Calendar**.
2. Choose **File → New Calendar Subscription**.
3. Paste the calendar URL above.
4. Select **Subscribe**.

### Google Calendar

1. Open Google Calendar.
2. Next to **Other calendars**, select **+**.
3. Choose **From URL**.
4. Paste the calendar URL above, then select **Add calendar**.

### Outlook Calendar

Please use **Outlook on the web**.

1. Open Outlook Calendar in your browser.
2. Select **Add calendar**.
3. Select **Subscribe from web**.
4. Paste the calendar URL above.
5. Name the calendar (for example, ASP or CRLA), then select **Import** or **Subscribe**.

If you subscribe to more than one calendar in Outlook, they may appear in separate columns. To view them together in one calendar grid, turn off **Split view** at the top of the calendar page.

> **Important:** Subscribe using the URL rather than downloading and importing the `.ics` file. Subscribing lets future updates sync automatically. Updates may not appear immediately, depending on your calendar app's refresh schedule.
