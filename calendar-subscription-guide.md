## Before you begin

1. Choose the calendar you want to add.
2. Copy its full URL above. On a computer, select the URL and use **Command + C** (Mac) or **Ctrl + C** (Windows). On a phone or tablet, press and hold the URL, then select **Copy**.
3. Open your preferred calendar app and follow the instructions below.

## How to subscribe

### Mac Calendar

1. Open the **Calendar** app on your Mac.
2. From the menu bar, select **File → New Calendar Subscription**.
3. Paste the copied calendar URL into the subscription field using **Command + V**.
4. Select **Subscribe**.
5. Choose a name, color, and refresh setting if prompted, then select **OK**.

The subscribed calendar will appear in the left sidebar under your calendar accounts.

### Google Calendar

1. Open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com) in a browser on a computer.
2. In the left sidebar, find **Other calendars** and select the **+** icon.
3. Choose **From URL**.
4. Paste the copied calendar URL into the **URL of calendar** field.
5. Select **Add calendar**.

After a moment, the calendar will appear under **Other calendars** in the left sidebar. Google Calendar may take some time to show new or updated events.

### Outlook Calendar

Please use **Outlook on the web** rather than the desktop app.

1. Open [Outlook Calendar](https://outlook.office.com/calendar/) in your browser.
2. Select **Add calendar** in the left sidebar.
3. Select **Subscribe from web**.
4. Paste the copied calendar URL into the URL field.
5. Enter a name for the calendar, such as **DKU Academic Calendar** or **CRLA Training**.
6. Select **Import** or **Subscribe**.

If you subscribe to more than one calendar, Outlook may show them in separate columns. To view all calendars together in one calendar grid, turn off **Split view** at the top of the calendar page.

## Important

Please **subscribe using the URL** instead of downloading and importing the `.ics` file. A subscription allows future updates to sync automatically. Updates may not appear immediately because each calendar app refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule.
